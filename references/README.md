# References

This directory holds research papers and books used to develop the
ecological footprint methodology. PDFs and EPUBs are **not tracked in git**
due to size and licensing.

## Key papers

| File | Citation |
|------|----------|
| `Borucke_et_al_2013.pdf` | Borucke et al. (2013). Accounting for demand and supply of the biosphere's regenerative capacity. *Ecological Indicators* 24. |
| `monfreda.pdf` | Monfreda et al. (2008). Farming the planet. *Global Biogeochemical Cycles*. |
| `resources-07-00058.pdf` | Lin et al. (2018). Ecological Footprint Accounting for Countries. *Resources* 7(58). |
| `sustainability-11-02164.pdf` | Mancini et al. (2019). Ecological Footprint and Sustainable Development. *Sustainability* 11. |
| `Tracking the Ecological Overshoot...pdf` | Wackernagel et al. (2002). Tracking the Ecological Overshoot of the Human Economy. *PNAS* 99(14). |
| `An_exploration_of_the_mathematics_behind.pdf` | Mathematical foundations of the Ecological Footprint. |
| `Measuring_Water.pdf` | Water footprint methodology reference. |
| `Ecological Footprint...epub` | Wackernagel & Beyers. *Ecological Footprint: Managing Our Biocapacity Budget*. |

## Converting to markdown

```bash
./scripts/convert_to_md.sh
# Output: references/md_output/
```

Requires `marker_single` (for PDFs) and `pandoc` (for EPUBs).
